Viterra implements SPAC in its Plants

During 2025, SPAC was implemented in Quequén (x2), Daireaux, and Ing. White Plants, all located in Buenos Aires province (Argentina); including new functionality for managing daily product quantities, interface with chromatographs for data acquisition, and issuance of COAs for finished product shipments. We will continue to share this new journey with Viterra, following a successful launch thanks to the great team involved in the project.

SPAC Users Meeting in Córdoba

After several years, on April 24, a new edition of the RuSPAC Intercompany 2025 was held at the Arcor Group auditorium (Córdoba - Argentina). Various success stories in the use of the system were shared, covering four blocks: 1) the use of SPAC in management (with suppliers, samples, and clients), 2) functionality for web portals and its use on mobile devices, 3) application of the system for process and environmental controls, and finally, 4) the evolution of SPAC in OpenID, UX, and Analytics.

New User Experience and SPai agent

The launch of a new user interface is imminent, aimed at modernizing and making the main screens of the desktop version of SPAC more user-friendly and functional. Highlights include a left-hand menu for access to static sections (structure setup) and a right-hand menu for access to dynamic sections (daily operations). Later, the first version of the new SPai agent (SPAC and AI) will be added, which can be used for system usage queries.

Savencia acquires SPAC for Milkaut

After acquiring the Ilolay brand along with its Bella Italia Plant (Santa Fe – Arg.), which has been using SPAC for several years, Savencia decided to implement SPAC at its Milkaut Plant in Franck (Santa Fe – Arg.), complemented with an interface to SAP for the release of Receipts and Finished Product. Transferring the solid knowledge and experience of the Bella Italia team, which relies daily on SPAC as an ally for quality management and technical specifications, along with process and environmental control and audit execution, among other functions.

GraphQL Interface to expose and insert data

The GraphQL https://spec.graphql.org/ standard was determined, and we are taking the first steps for SPAC to expose information that will eventually consume a BI application, for example; and to provide a data entry point into the system. Initially, for the TEST entity, one of the main ones in SPAC, and later to be extended to Audits, Non-Conformities, Technical Specifications, and Quality Indices.
