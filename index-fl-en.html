objectives

Offering support for projects of Statistical Engineering.

Providing immediate access to the right statistical tool for a job and assistance in its interpretation.

Facilitate Data Analysis by intuitive graphical tools, which could be complemented by rigorous analytical support.

Friendly user interface that speeds up the learning curve.

Seemless integration with MS Excel®, MS Word®, E-mail, Druida SPAC®, etc. to import/export data or to generate reports.
